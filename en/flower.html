Packaging Inquiry
01 · CHARACTERISTICS

Why cut flowers lose freshness.

Water loss

Petals and leaves can lose moisture and firmness over time. Stem water uptake is also important.

Temperature & time

Higher or fluctuating temperatures may accelerate water loss and deterioration. Suitable temperatures vary by species.

Handling & condensation

Bruising, friction and excess condensation can compromise appearance and increase microbiological risks.

02 · EXISTING CARE

Storage and transport must work together.

Maintain an appropriate water supply, follow variety-specific temperature guidelines and use protective packaging structures to reduce crushing or abrasion during handling.

03 · TERRASAVE APPROACH

Evaluate packaging by flower variety and route.

We review the flower type, cut-stem length, harvesting or cutting time, required moisture balance, ventilation, condensation risk and shipment method. Any claimed improvement requires validation under defined test conditions.

04 · RESEARCH

Observe and compare changes over time.

Comparison records of yellow spray roses illustrate changes in appearance at different observation points. Visual examples should not be treated as verified performance results without the relevant test records.

05 · INFORMATION TO PROVIDE

What helps us evaluate your application.

Flower variety

Species, cutting conditions, stem length and observed quality issues.

Distribution environment

Storage temperature, humidity, transport duration and use of cold-chain logistics.

Success criteria

Petal firmness, color, wilting, moisture and comparable observation points.
