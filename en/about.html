ABOUT TERRASAVE

Keep Fresh.
Save Loss.

Protecting freshness means understanding what happens between harvest, storage, transportation and the moment of purchase.

THE CHALLENGE

Fresh produce is sensitive to its entire journey.

Fruit, vegetables and flowers face different risks during distribution. Ripening, moisture loss, condensation, temperature changes and physical damage can shorten marketable life. TerraSAVE begins with the actual conditions of each product rather than treating spoilage as a single problem.

OUR APPROACH

Packaging designed around each crop.

Understand the product

Identify crop-specific deterioration factors and relevant storage conditions.

Match the packaging

Examine protective structures, material formats and the packaging environment.

Validate in practice

Evaluate results using comparable observations and distribution data before making performance claims.

OUR PARTNERS

Protecting value for the businesses that grow and sell.

Our partners include producers, exporters, distributors, packaging manufacturers and retailers. We seek to reduce unnecessary waste while supporting commercial quality, shipment planning and flexibility across the supply chain.

RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Evidence-led development for specific supply chains.

We assess product variety, packaging form, storage and shipping temperature, current distribution period, desired extension, observed quality issues and return or disposal rates. These inputs help define an appropriate development and verification plan.
