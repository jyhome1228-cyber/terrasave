FLORAL FRESHNESS

Flowers and cut flowers need different care.

Petal condition, water uptake, moisture loss, temperature and transport damage all influence flower quality. The appropriate packaging approach depends on flower variety and distribution conditions.

HOW TO READ

Three factors to consider.

Temperature & humidity

Balance the basic conditions for quality maintenance with cold injury and moisture-loss risks.

Ripening & ethylene

Distinguish ethylene production, sensitivity and changes after harvest.

Distribution risks

Review bruising, softening, condensation, mold and other real-world damage factors.
