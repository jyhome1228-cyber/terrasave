WHY TERRASAVE

Freshness can create
more possibilities.

Packaging is more than a container. It can influence which markets you reach, how products move and how much value survives the journey.

VALUE BY BUSINESS

Different partners. Different priorities.

Exporters

Study ways to protect quality on longer routes, assess transport alternatives and reduce arrival-related claims.

Packaging & Distribution Operators

Manage damage, waste, stock turnover and handling costs with crop-specific packaging decisions.

Retailers & Direct Sellers

Maintain attractive product presentation, reduce avoidable disposal and extend selling opportunities where validated.

Global Distribution Brands

Evaluate consistent packaging systems, product-loss metrics and quality across suppliers and regions.

REAL-WORLD APPLICATION

Better choices across the journey.

Flexible dispatch

If marketable quality can be maintained, shipment and inventory decisions may become more flexible.

Operational efficiency

Packaging designed for specific crops may reduce repeated sorting, repacking and unnecessary handling.

Customer confidence

Present produce clearly while managing moisture, appearance and transport-related damage.

MEASURE WHAT MATTERS

Performance should be demonstrated.

The most useful evaluation compares equivalent conditions: firmness, color, weight change, condensation, spoilage, visual quality and marketable life. Outcomes depend on the produce, packaging material, environmental conditions and test design; no universal extension is guaranteed.
