PARTNERSHIP

Beyond product supply.
Solutions designed together.

From packaging customization to joint research and market licensing, our collaboration model is shaped by each partner's commercial goals.

COLLABORATION MODELS

Choose the depth of partnership.

Custom Product Development

Adapt materials, formats and specifications to a target crop, distribution environment and packaging manufacturing partner.

Joint Research & Development

Combine TerraSAVE capabilities with partner product and distribution data to study and validate new preservation approaches.

Exclusive Licensing

Define commercial rights by geography, crop, market and scope, subject to negotiated licensing terms.

CUSTOM DEVELOPMENT

Designed around real packaging operations.

We can review crops, required packaging formats, target shelf life, production quantities and existing manufacturing relationships. Partners may specify a preferred masterbatch or packaging manufacturer where feasible.

JOINT R&D

What collaborative research can examine.

Shelf-life conditions

Review current and target distribution periods using comparable measurements.

Moisture and headspace

Study condensation, moisture loss and packaging microenvironment, including O₂ and CO₂ conditions where relevant.

Quality verification

Compare firmness, color, weight loss, external appearance, spoilage and marketable life.

Research outcomes, intellectual property and commercialization rights are separately negotiated.

LICENSING

A defined route to market.

Exclusive use can be structured for specified countries, regions or fresh-produce categories. Commercial terms may include licensing fees, sales- or volume-based royalties and clearly defined rights and obligations.

OUR PROCESS

03 · Commercial Terms

Define supply, licensing, rights, fees and implementation arrangements.
